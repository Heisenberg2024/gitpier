## Design decisions

- Follow Apple's [Designing for macOS](https://developer.apple.com/design/human-interface-guidelines/designing-for-macos/) and [Materials](https://developer.apple.com/design/human-interface-guidelines/materials) guidance. Keep the system unified window toolbar and native Sheets, Popovers, menus and Alerts. The sidebar, reading and editing panes use opaque system colors; the 2026-11-04 sidebar follow-up restores its earlier solid background. Use newer corner APIs only when both compiler and runtime support them. See [Modernize your AppKit app](https://developer.apple.com/videos/play/wwdc2026/289/).
- Buttons, focus, selected rows, section titles, panel edges and outcome feedback share semantic colors and metrics. Accent does not replace text or symbols. Keep a static indicator for indeterminate progress with Reduce Motion.
- Keep the existing `UnifiedScrollbars`, `OverlayScrollbars` and `OverlayScrollThumb` behavior. Native presentation roots must install `AppAppearance`; Popovers retain their native background and `AppPopoverAppearance`.

### Previous measured-motion correction (historical)

Push, Commit & Push and other transfers used one left-anchored fill. `OperationProgressValue` retains the measured object-writing fraction for Push and object-receiving fraction for Fetch/Clone; preparatory counting/compression never completes the bar, and later phases/refresh never reset it. No timer or step weighting invents a percentage. Transfers wait at zero until a measurement arrives, remain below full while the final result is pending, and reach full only on success. Failure, cancellation and unknown outcomes stop at the last measured value; verified Push readback can still complete the matching record.
